* Human review seems to be the bottleneck for AI-generated code, it's time to rethink the traditional PR workflow. No or less human in the loop.
* Kubernetes is a good platform for long-running workloads like microservices and batch processing. But not ideal for short-lived ones like agents today, which require strong isolation, fast snapshot/resume, and rapid scaling up/down. Time to think about how to build a platform for agents with microVMs. Unified Kubernetes platform sounds interesting, no more fragmented infrastructure, but also challenging as a different model. As well as the GPU support.
* Personal agents are the next product phenomenon. But ideally they should be deployed locally for privacy, low latency, and offline capabilities.
* Local AI (including software and hardware stack) will be the key enabler for personal agents. Also a big thing for AI infrastructure going forward.
* Every company should hire someone working on AI for workflow automation just like the way they hire for DevOps or SecOps.
* With AI, everyone is a researcher today, since agent is quite good at engineering tasks.
* Writing code is no longer the bottleneck in software development, but verification is. Learn to write formal verification to ensure correctness and benchmark for performance.
* Software is no longer the moat for companies, hardware is holding up slightly better than software.
* AI safety is a super big market and challenge.
